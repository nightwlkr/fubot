# OBFVG 45-Day Validation — 2026-11-05

**Purpose**: Task 15 of `analysis/trade-loss-fix-tasks.md` — OBFVG was the only strategy
with a positive backtest (**+0.315R/trade**, 84 trades over **3.5 days**, 20 symbols).
That sample is below the 20-trades-per-cell bar in `analysis/AGENTS.md`. This run
re-measures OBFVG over 45 days of paginated data and decides whether it has a genuine
edge worth enabling.

**Harness**: `scripts/backtest.py` → `backend/backtest.py`. OBFVG is replayed through the
**production detector** (`backend/services/signal_service.py:58 detect_obfvg_candidates`)
on closed candle prefixes — no lookahead. Data is paginated by `backend/backtest_data.py`
and all three intervals are pinned to ONE `[start, end)` window; the run aborts if any
interval returns fewer than 90% of its expected bar count (`backend/backtest.py:758`).

**Windows** (from `window_for_days(45)`, anchored to the last closed 5m bar):

| Label | Start | End | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Recent** | 2026-09-21 05:05 UTC | 2026-11-05 05:05 UTC | `--days 45` |
| **Earlier** | 2026-08-07 05:05 UTC | 2026-09-21 05:05 UTC | `--days 45 --end-days-ago 45` |

The two windows are **exactly contiguous and disjoint** — together they cover 90 days.
The earlier window was added because the previous OBFVG comparison (`analysis/panel/DECISION.md:1376`,
2026-10-23) found two disjoint 60-day windows **disagreeing on the candidate's sign**;
a single window cannot settle a candidate that has already shown sign instability.

**Fee model**: 0.11% round trip (0.045% taker ×2 + 0.01% slippage ×2,
`backend/core/cost_model.py`). `netR` / `expNetR` are the trustworthy columns;
`totalR` / `expR` are gross.

**Shared config** (deployed production settings, `backend/routers/settings.py:86-119`):

```
rr_min=3.0   trade_min_trend_strength=MODERATE   trade_neutral_markets=true
volume_filter_enabled=false   regime_filter_enabled=true   signal_max_age_minutes=60
min_risk_pct=0.005 (code default)   atr_stop_mult=1.5 (3CANDLE-only; does not affect OBFVG)

be_trail_trigger_r=0.5   profit_lock_trigger_r=1.0   profit_lock_trail_r=0.5
```

`atr_stop_mult` is a 3CANDLE knob only — it is applied inside
`evaluate_three_candle_entry` and never touches OBFVG. OBFVG stops come from
`evaluate_fvg_entry` (`backend/indicators/fvg.py:98`): the FVG zone edge **± 1h ATR(14)**,
with the target at `rr_min` × that distance.

**No run required a retry** — all six CLI runs and the probe completed on the first
attempt (no transient Binance errors).

## The central finding — OBFVG's sign is not stable

**Every per-symbol cell in every run except two is below 20 trades and is therefore
inconclusive by the `analysis/AGENTS.md` evidence standard.** The TOTAL rows are the
readable ones, and they flip:

| Config | Universe | Recent 45d | Earlier 45d | Pooled 90d |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OBFVG only | 7-symbol research | **-0.384** (76) | **+0.132** (86) | **-0.110** (162) |
| OBFVG only | Enabled (5) | **-0.040** (58) | **+0.149** (67) | **+0.062** (125) |

Pooled totals:

| Universe | trades | gross R | net R | expNetR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 7-symbol research | 162 | **-0.33** | -17.84 | **-0.110** |
| Enabled (5) | 125 | +18.88 | +7.69 | +0.062 |

**On the 7-symbol research universe the pooled gross result over 90 days is -0.33R on 162
trades — statistically indistinguishable from zero.** The entire loss is the fee drag:
**17.51R of costs over 162 trades** (0.108 R/trade averaged over the pooled sample; 0.094
R/trade median in the recent window) turns a zero-edge strategy into -17.84R net. This is
the same shape as the 3CANDLE finding in `analysis/backtest-45d-2026-11-05.md`: the
strategy has no measurable gross edge, and fees are the difference between flat and
negative.

### Statistical test

A throwaway probe re-fetched the recent 45d window and replayed the same OBFVG-only
config, keeping per-trade net R so the mean could be tested rather than just quoted:

| Universe | trades | mean net R | sd | t | 95% CI (bootstrap, 20k) | distinguishable from 0? |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| 7-symbol research | 77 | -0.394 | 0.865 | **-4.00** | **[-0.577, -0.196]** | **yes** |
| Enabled (5) | 58 | -0.040 | 1.109 | -0.27 | [-0.314, +0.253] | no |

The 7-symbol result is **statistically significant and negative** (t = -4.00, CI excludes
zero). The enabled-universe result is **indistinguishable from zero** — it is not
evidence of an edge, it is evidence of *not enough signal*.

(The probe's 77 trades vs the CLI's 76 is the documented window-anchor effect:
`window_for_days` ends at the last closed 5m bar *at wall-clock time*, so a run four
minutes later includes one more boundary trade. `analysis/backtest-45d-2026-11-05.md`
records the same 1-trade drift between processes.)

### Why OBFVG loses — exit-reason breakdown

From the probe, per-trade exit reasons:

| Universe | SL_HIT | BE_STOP | TP_HIT | win/loss/breakeven | median stop % of entry | median fee drag |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 7-symbol research | 37 | 38 | **2** | 18 / 43 / 16 | 1.171% | 0.094 R |
| Enabled (5) | 23 | 32 | **3** | 22 / 25 / 11 | 1.233% | 0.092 R |

**Only 2 of 77 trades reached the 3R target.** The population is overwhelmingly
stop-outs and break-even scratches: OBFVG's 1h FVG geometry produces a wide risk
distance (median 1.17% of entry = gap half-width + 1h ATR) and a target 3× that
distance away, which almost never gets hit. Break-even trailing at +0.5R converts most
winners into scratches, capping the payoff at roughly +0.5R while the loss is the full
-1R. At a 23% win rate and ~2.6% target-hit rate the distribution has no way to pay for
itself.

This is also why the fee drag is *not* the dominant problem for OBFVG (0.094 R/trade,
comfortably inside the "fee drag < 0.3R" Phase-1 success criterion). Unlike 3CANDLE,
**OBFVG's problem is not fees — it is that it does not pay out.**

### Combined run (OBFVG + 3CANDLE) vs the 3CANDLE-only control

| Config | trades | gross R | net R | expNetR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3CANDLE only (run 5) | 452 | +29.73 | -12.25 | **-0.027** |
| OBFVG + 3CANDLE (run 3) | 474 | +14.39 | -30.46 | **-0.064** |
| delta | **+22** | **-15.34** | **-18.21** | -0.037 |

Adding OBFVG to the mix added **22 fills** and cost **18.21R net**, an implied marginal
cost of **-0.83R per added trade**.

**This delta is not a clean attribution** and must not be read as one. The harness
serialises **one position per symbol** (`backend/backtest.py:608`), and candidates are
appended in the order FVG → 3CANDLE → CHOC → OBFVG (`backend/backtest.py:375-440`), so a
3CANDLE candidate takes the slot before OBFVG is considered in the same scan bar. An
OBFVG fill therefore displaces a 3CANDLE trade that would otherwise have taken the same
slot, and the two are not separable in this replay. Live does **not** have this
starvation property — `scan_symbol` emits each strategy's candidates as independent
signals and the one-position check happens later, at execution. **The combined run
therefore cannot answer "does OBFVG add value to 3CANDLE"; only the OBFVG-only runs can,
and they are the ones that speak to this task.**

## Verdict

**OBFVG does not have a demonstrated, stable edge. Do not enable it.**

1. **The headline number does not survive a longer window.** The claim that motivated
   Task 14 and Task 15 — "+0.315R/trade, the only strategy with a positive backtest" —
   came from 84 trades over 3.5 days on 20 symbols. At 45 days on the 7-symbol research
   universe the same strategy measures **-0.384 expNetR (76 trades)**, and the result is
   **statistically significant (t = -4.00, 95% CI [-0.577, -0.196])**. The original figure
   is not reproduced.

2. **Pooled over 90 days on the research universe, OBFVG's gross edge is zero**
   (-0.33R over 162 trades). Fees take 17.51R from a strategy that had none to give.

3. **The sign flips between adjacent 45-day windows on both universes**
   (-0.384 → +0.132 on the research set; -0.040 → +0.149 on the enabled set), and four
   of seven symbols flip individually. A strategy whose sign depends on which 45 days
   you happened to sample is a regime artefact, not an edge. This is an independent
   reproduction of the 2026-10-23 finding on longer windows.

4. **The mechanism is disqualifying.** Only 2 of 77 trades hit the 3R target; the
   population is 37 stop-outs and 38 break-even scratches. OBFVG does not fail because
   costs are too high (fee drag 0.094 R/trade is comfortable); it fails because its
   1h-FVG geometry — a wide zone-edge ± ATR stop with a target 3× away — almost never
   pays out, and break-even trailing then caps the winners at ~+0.5R against a full -1R
   loss.

5. **Per-symbol cells cannot carry a decision.** In the OBFVG-only runs every cell is
   under 20 trades except UNIUSDT in the earlier window (20 trades on the research
   universe, 21 on the enabled universe). UNIUSDT is the best cell measured (+0.451)
   and also the most sign-unstable (+0.451 earlier → -0.218 recent). There is no
   symbol-level OBFVG configuration to adopt.

**On the enabled universe specifically** the pooled 90-day number is mildly positive
(+0.062 expNetR, +7.69R over 125 trades), but this is **not** sufficient to enable it:
the two windows disagree in sign, the recent window's result is statistically
indistinguishable from zero (t = -0.27), the pooled total is driven almost entirely by
**one symbol** — UNIUSDT contributes +7.51R of the +7.69R (ARBUSDT is the only negative
contributor at -2.54R, the other four are between +0.50R and +1.58R) — and the whole
sample sits alongside the 7-symbol research universe's -0.110. Enabling a candidate
whose measured expectancy ranges from -0.384 to +0.149 depending on the window is not
"validated" — it is unmeasured.

**Recommendation** (for the Lead/user to decide — nothing was changed):

- **Keep `strategy_obfvg_enabled = false` in production.** No production setting,
  order, or service was touched by this task.
- **Task 14 (enable OBFVG for paper testing) loses its stated rationale** — "only
  strategy with positive backtest (+0.315R/trade)" is now falsified. Paper mode costs
  nothing, so a paper-only observation run is still defensible, but it must be
  justified as *gathering* evidence, not as *extending* a validated edge. If it is run,
  it must be monitored for the 2 weeks Task 14 specifies and judged against the same
  20-trade floor.
- **Do not pursue OBFVG further until the exit path is re-measured at 5m resolution**
  (see caveats). The current harness resolves OBFVG exits on hourly bars; the natural
  follow-up is a 5m-resolution OBFVG exit replay, which is the one change that could
  plausibly raise the TP-hit rate.

## Caveats — read these before quoting any number above

1. **OBFVG entries AND exits resolve on the 1h series**; 3CANDLE's resolve on 5m. OBFVG
   candidates carry `tf="1h"` (`backend/backtest.py:438`), so `fill_at_midpoint` scans
   1h bars and `_resolve_exit` walks 1h bars. Two consequences:
   - **Only the hour immediately after the gap formed can fill** (the 60-minute signal
     expiry expires at the end of that hour), and only if price revisits the zone
     midpoint. This is why `no_fill` is the third-largest skip bucket (215 in run 1,
     behind counter_trend=279 and ranging_regime=226).
   - **The exit is hourly, with stop-before-target within-bar ordering.** With a median
     stop of 1.17% of entry and a 3R target, an hourly bar that spans both always
     resolves as the stop. This **systematically penalises OBFVG relative to 3CANDLE**
     and may understate OBFVG's absolute level. It is, however, the *same* harness used
     for the 2026-10-23 OBFVG comparison, so window-to-window comparisons here are
     like-for-like.
2. **No queue/partial-fill modelling.** Touch-fill baseline (`entry_penetration_bps=0`);
   the 1bp sensitivity run in `analysis/backtest-45d-2026-11-05.md` moved expectancy by
   <0.01R there, so this is unlikely to flip a conclusion.
3. **The harness omits `PositionManager._check_profit_trailing_2`** (prod default 1.0R
   trigger / 0.25R trail) — the second-stage trail that locks slightly tighter than the
   first-stage 0.5R trail the harness does model. It only affects trades that reached
   +1R, which are rare in the OBFVG population (only 2 of 77 reached +3R), so the
   effect on these numbers is small and not directional.
4. **No portfolio simulation.** One position per symbol, serialised; no shared
   collateral, no three-slot cap, no `max_daily_trades`, and the historical funding-rate
   filter is not applied (production has `funding_rate_filter_enabled=true`).
5. **`profit_lock_trail_r`**: harness 0.5, prod default 0.5, but the production settings
   row may hold 0.75 (`analysis/backtest-45d-2026-11-05.md` notes this). Identical across
   every run here, so it does not affect the comparisons.
6. **`min_risk_pct = 0.005` applies to OBFVG too** and removed 47 candidates in run 1
   (BNBUSDT 27, BTCUSDT 20). The deployed default binds.
7. **Two 45-day windows is two regime samples, not a distribution.** The 90-day pooled
   figure is the most honest single number available here (-0.110 on the research
   universe), and even that spans one bull and one chop.
8. **The 20-trade floor is applied per cell.** No per-symbol OBFVG configuration is
   supported by this data at any sample size.
